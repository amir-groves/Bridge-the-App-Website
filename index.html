HOW IT WORKS

01

CROSSED PATHS

Proximity tech detects when you've been near another student — in a lecture, the library, or anywhere on campus. Connect afterwards, even if you didn't speak in the moment.

02

BROADCASTS

Send a real-time message to the student community around you. Organise a study session, find event partners, or recruit teammates — no group chat needed.

03

PEOPLE FINDER

Search for students by course, interests, and goals. Find a study partner for a specific module, a collaborator for a project, or simply meet like-minded people outside your flat or course group.
